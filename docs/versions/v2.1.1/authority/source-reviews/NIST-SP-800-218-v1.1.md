NIST states that SP 800-218A augments SP 800-218 v1.1 with practices, tasks, recommendations, considerations, notes and informative references specific to AI model development throughout the SDLC, and that the profile should be used in conjunction with SP 800-218. It is a distinct companion source, not a replacement or an implicit part of the v1.1 text.

## 3. Authority fit and limits

SP 800-218 is a NIST-published recommended-practice framework, not an ISO/IEC/IEEE international standard. It is appropriate as:
- a government-published reference for high-level secure software development practices;
- a source for the SSDF v1.1 practice groups, tasks and intended outcomes;
- a source for NIST's own stated guidance about risk-based adoption and SDLC integration.

It is not:
- proof that the practices cause a quantified reduction in vulnerabilities in every context;
- a mandatory universal checklist for every organization;
- a detailed implementation recipe for every toolchain or development method;
- a complete taxonomy of cybersecurity or Software Engineering;
- a complete AI-model security framework without its separately published companion profile.

NIST explicitly says the framework is a high-level subset, not all that an organization may need; not all practices apply to all use cases; and risk, cost, feasibility and applicability should inform selection. It also says the order of practices/tasks/examples does not imply implementation sequence or relative importance.

## 5. Currentness and companion-profile check

The NIST SSDF project page currently lists SP 800-218A as finalized and describes it as augmenting SP 800-218 for generative AI and dual-use foundation models. The official SP 800-218A publication page confirms final publication on 2024-07-26 and says it should be used with SP 800-218.

This has a direct boundary implication: AI-enabled software engineering and AI model development are not the same scope. The corpus may use SP 800-218 for baseline secure software development and must separately evaluate SP 800-218A for AI-model development. Do not infer that SP 800-218 v1.1 alone fully covers AI model-specific secure development.

## 6. Rights and handling

The SP 800-218 PDF states that the publication may be used by nongovernmental organizations on a voluntary basis and is not subject to copyright in the United States; attribution is appreciated. This does not remove the need to cite the source accurately or to check rights for any third-party referenced material. The corpus stores concise original paraphrases and locators, not long reproductions.
