   - Tools and Environments

   - Refactoring and Code Evolution
   - Software Reliability

3. The SE-Tools unit covers software configuration management/version control, release management, testing tools and software process automation/continuous integration.
   - Locator: Body of Knowledge PDF, Software Engineering section, printed page 170, SE-Tools.

## Interpretation limits

- CS2023 is a curriculum framework. It structures what a CS curriculum should cover; it does not claim to be a complete professional Software Engineering body of knowledge.
- The nine SE Knowledge Units are not equivalent to the 18 SWEBOK Knowledge Areas. They are a different classification at a different granularity and for a different purpose.
- The presence of a KU is evidence of curricular inclusion, not evidence of universal professional scope or effectiveness.
- The report also places related topics such as professional ethics in other CS curriculum areas. Cross-area references should be preserved rather than forced into the SE area.
