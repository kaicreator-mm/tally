## Verified facts and locators

1. The Guide's Table I.1 enumerates 18 Knowledge Areas.
   - Locator: printed page xxxix, Table I.1, “The 18 SWEBOK KAs”.
2. Table I.2 identifies eleven related disciplines.
   - Locator: printed page xxxix, Table I.2, “Related Disciplines”.
3. The Guide describes a hierarchical organization of topics, typically two or three levels.
   - Locator: printed page xl, “Hierarchical Organization”.
4. The Guide distinguishes the Guide from the body of knowledge itself and states limits on comprehensiveness and uniform applicability.
   - Locator: printed pages xxxvii–xli, Introduction to the Guide, objectives/scope and “Depth of Treatment”.
5. The Guide identifies related disciplines but does not attempt to characterize the knowledge of those disciplines in full.
   - Locator: printed pages xxxviii–xxxix, related-discipline discussion and Table I.2.
